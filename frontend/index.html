Lab Report Analyzer

Phase 2 · Extract · Compare · Review · Ask follow-up questions

1. Analyze a lab report

PDF/image upload (10 MB maximum; PDFs use page 1 only). Real extraction requires MedGemma enabled on the server.

Saving is optional. Use synthetic/sample reports until privacy controls are reviewed.

2. Compare two saved reports

Only consented reports appear. Different/missing units are flagged; the app never guesses conversions.

3. Follow-up question

Answers are grounded in saved report values and example curated notes, not free-form medical advice.

4. Biomarker chart

Shows one extracted value and its reported reference interval. Use comparison for two reports.

Safety and guardrails

This prototype does not diagnose, recommend treatment, or replace a clinician. Verify every value, unit, and reference range against the original report. Do not upload real patient data before privacy/security review.
